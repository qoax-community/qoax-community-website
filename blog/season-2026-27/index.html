The 2026/27 season: six events, one calendar

After Atanasoff48 and SUGAMING Jam in October, the season continues with six events at schools and universities in Sofia.

Q
Qoax Community
Published
4 November 2026

On this page

  1. The calendar
  2. How the pieces connect
  3. Joining

The calendar

Dates marked as working or under discussion can still move. Each event page is updated as soon as something is confirmed.

  • 20–22 Nov 2026 · [Sofia Game Jam](/projects/sofia-game-jam-2026/). Theme: “Arcade with a Mission”, games for arcade machines that take bottles and cans. Qoax runs the livestream and helps bring in sponsors.
  • End of Nov 2026 · [TUES Game Jam](/projects/tues-game-jam-2026/). Theme: “It’s not supposed to do that!” Teams of three to six, with at least one 8th-grader in every team. Exact dates to be confirmed.
  • Jan–Feb 2027 · [Atanasoff Jam](/projects/john-atanasoff-game-jam-2026/). The game jam of SPGE John Atanasoff. 31 January – 2 February is under discussion.
  • 27–28 Feb and 6–7 Mar 2027 · [Qoax × SUGAMING Gaming Tournament](/projects/fmi-esports-tournament-2027/). Counter-Strike 2 and League of Legends: online qualifiers, then finals with an audience at FMI, one day per game. Working dates.
  • 4 Mar 2027 · [Open day at John Atanasoff](/projects/john-atanasoff-open-day-2027/). Visitors try games and prototypes made by students.
  • 26–28 Mar 2027 · [Telecommunications Hackathon](/projects/telecommunications-hackathon-2027/). At the Professional High School of Telecommunications. Theme: technology for healthcare.

How the pieces connect

Most of the season is game jams, because a game is a small, complete product that a team can finish in a weekend. The open day in March then puts games and prototypes made at the school in front of future students.

The tournament and the hackathon widen the circle: one brings players and an audience to FMI, the other asks teams to build for healthcare.

Joining

Schools, student clubs, and universities that want to send teams or help organize can write to contact@qo.ax. The full list is on the Events page.

Сезон 2026/27: Sofia Game Jam (20–22 ноември), TUES Game Jam (края на ноември), Atanasoff Jam (януари–февруари), турнир с SUGAMING (27–28 февруари и 6–7 март), ден на отворените врати в СПГЕ „Джон Атанасов“ (4 март) и хакатон в ПГ по телекомуникации (26–28 март).
