Atanasoff48: the first 48-hour hackathon at SPGE John Atanasoff

From 2 to 4 October 2026, the school's STEM Centre hosted its first 48-hour hackathon, organized by the Student Council.

What it was

Atanasoff48 is the hackathon of SPGE John Atanasoff, organized by the school's Student Council. The first edition ran from 2 to 4 October 2026 at the school's STEM Centre in Sofia.

  • 62 participants
  • 16 teams
  • 48 hours to build a project and present it, followed by the awards

Who did what

The event belongs to the Student Council. Qoax is part of the team behind its digital presence and helps with practical delivery. The official site is atanasoff48.com.

That split is how we prefer to work with schools: the students own the event and the decisions, and we bring the parts that are hard to build from scratch every year.

What comes next

A second edition is planned for October–November 2027. It will be prepared with its own team, budget, and partner programme. Dates will be published on the event record once they are confirmed.

Before that, the school hosts Atanasoff Jam, a game jam planned for January–February 2027.

Atanasoff48 е хакатонът на СПГЕ „Джон Атанасов“, организиран от Ученическия съвет. Първото издание се проведе от 2 до 4 октомври 2026 г.: 62 участници в 16 отбора имаха 48 часа, за да създадат и представят проект.
