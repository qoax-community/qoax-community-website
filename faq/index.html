The name

Is Коакс Комюнити the same as Qoax Community?

Yes. Сдружение КОАКС КОМЮНИТИ is the registered Bulgarian name of the association, and QOAX COMMUNITY association is its English name. Both are the organisation behind qo.ax, with UIC (ЕИК) 208896893.

The community

What is Qoax Community?

Qoax Community is an independent non-profit technology community based in Sofia, Bulgaria. We run hackathons, game jams, and tournaments with schools and universities, mentor students through the summer internship programme, and build software for civic and cultural organisations.

Everything we do is public work: each event and project has a record page, and the journal explains what was planned, what happened, and what we would change.

Is Qoax a company?

No. Qoax Community is a registered non-profit association, UIC (ЕИК) 208896893, registered in Sofia on 24 July 2026. There is no commercial catalogue and no advertising on this site.

The full registration details are on the About page.

Who is behind Qoax?

Engineers, teachers, and students in Sofia. We will introduce the people behind the community in the journal in the coming days; every post carries the name of the person who wrote it.

Events and schools

What events does Qoax run?

In the 2026–2027 season: Atanasoff48, a 48-hour hackathon at SPGE John Atanasoff on 2–4 October 2026; the John Atanasoff Game Jam planned for December 2026; the Telecommunications School Hackathon planned for March–April 2027; the completed FMI Game Jam at the Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics, Sofia University, with photos coming in the next few days; the Qoax × SUGAMING Gaming Tournament in early March 2027; and the summer Internship Programme for students from partner schools. Exact dates and participation details for the two planned school events will be announced once confirmed.

Every event has a record page with dates, partners, and what we are responsible for. See all events.

Can my school or student council run an event with Qoax?

Yes. Write to contact@qo.ax with what you have in mind: a hackathon, a game jam, a tournament, or a workshop. We plan it together with the school and publish a record page for it, as we did for Atanasoff48.

Which schools work with Qoax?

Technology schools in Sofia whose students took part in the 2026 internship programme or whose councils run events with us. The current list, with logos and notes, is on the Schools page.

Working with us

Do you build software for NGOs and artists?

Yes, for public-interest organisations: NGO websites, theatre installations, and civic tools. We build things the organisation can keep running after we step back, and we publish each as a record. See our work.

Is your work open source?

Our code is published under the qoax-community organisation on GitHub.

How do I contact Qoax?

Write to contact@qo.ax. We reply to every message.

Still have a question?

Write to contact@qo.ax. We reply to every message, and the questions people ask most end up on this page.
