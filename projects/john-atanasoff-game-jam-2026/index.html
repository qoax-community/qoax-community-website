About the event

Atanasoff Jam is the game jam of SPGE John Atanasoff. Teams make a game together with support from mentors, then present the finished projects. Qoax handles logistics, design, sponsors, and social media.

31 January – 2 February 2027 is under discussion. Confirmed dates will be published here.
