About the event

SUGAMING Game Jam was organized by the SUGAMING club and held from 16 to 18 October 2026 at the Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics, Sofia University. Teams built a game over the weekend and presented it at the end. Seven games were submitted.

The weekend

  • The theme, Chaotic Business, was announced at the start. Submissions on itch.io ran from 16 to 18 October.
  • Seven games were submitted. All of them are listed on the jam’s itch.io page.
  • On the last day every team presented its game, and the Best Game award was given.
