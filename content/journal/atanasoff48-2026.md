## What it was

Atanasoff48 is the hackathon of SPGE John Atanasoff, organized by the school's Student Council. The first edition ran from 2 to 4 October 2026 at the school's STEM Centre in Sofia.

- **62** participants
- **16** teams
- **48** hours to build a project and present it, followed by the awards

## Who did what

The event belongs to the Student Council. Qoax is part of the team behind its digital presence and helps with practical delivery. The official site is [atanasoff48.com](https://atanasoff48.com/).

That split is how we prefer to work with schools: the students own the event and the decisions, and we bring the parts that are hard to build from scratch every year.

## What comes next

A second edition is planned for October to November 2027. It will be prepared with its own team, budget, and partner programme. Dates will be published on the [event record](/projects/atanasoff48/) once they are confirmed.

Before that, the school hosts [Atanasoff Jam](/projects/john-atanasoff-game-jam-2026/), a game jam planned for January to February 2027.
