## The calendar

Dates marked as working or under discussion can still move. Each event page is updated as soon as something is confirmed.

- **[NASA Space Apps Challenge Sofia](/projects/nasa-space-apps-2026/)**, 14-15 Nov 2026. The Sofia local event of the global hackathon, at the Incubator in Sofia Tech Park. Theme: “The Next Frontier”. Free, teams of up to six.
- **[Sofia Game Jam](/projects/sofia-game-jam-2026/)**, 20-22 Nov 2026. Theme: “Arcade with a Mission”, games for arcade machines that take bottles and cans. Qoax runs the livestream and helps bring in sponsors.
- **[TUES Game Jam](/projects/tues-game-jam-2026/)**, End of Nov 2026. Teams of three to six, with at least one 8th-grader in every team. Exact dates to be confirmed.
- **[Atanasoff Jam](/projects/john-atanasoff-game-jam-2026/)**, Jan-Feb 2027. The game jam of SPGE John Atanasoff. 31 January to 2 February is under discussion.
- **[Qoax × SUGAMING Gaming Tournament](/projects/fmi-esports-tournament-2027/)**, 27-28 Feb and 6-7 Mar 2027. Counter-Strike 2 and League of Legends: online qualifiers, then finals with an audience at FMI, one day per game. Working dates.
- **[Open day at John Atanasoff](/projects/john-atanasoff-open-day-2027/)**, 4 Mar 2027. Visitors try games and prototypes made by students.
- **[Telecommunications Hackathon](/projects/telecommunications-hackathon-2027/)**, 26-28 Mar 2027. At the Professional High School of Telecommunications. Theme: technology for healthcare.

## How the pieces connect

Most of the season is game jams, because a game is a small, complete product that a team can finish in a weekend. The open day in March then puts games and prototypes made at the school in front of future students.

The tournament and the hackathon widen the circle: one brings players and an audience to FMI, the other asks teams to build for healthcare.

## Joining

Schools, student clubs, and universities that want to send teams or help organize can write to [contact@qo.ax](mailto:contact@qo.ax). The full list is on the [Events](/events/) page.
